# Exact canonical/strict Selmer dimensions over \(K=\mathbf Q(\sqrt{-3})\), and the real derived-Kato obstruction

Date: 2026-11-06. A structural, all-good-odd-p cohomology proof. **Does not rerun earlier certificates.**

Let E=389a1, E(Q)=Z², analytic/algebraic rank 2. Let d=-3, K=Q(sqrt(d)); the twist E^d has certified nonzero central L-value, hence E^d(Q) has rank 0 and Sha(E^d/Q) is finite. Fix any good prime p≥5 (excluding p=389). Write V=V_pE, V^-=V_pE^d and let
\[
t_p=\operatorname{corank}_{Z_p}\Sha(E/Q)[p^\infty]\in\mathbf Z_{\ge0}.
\]

For any number field F define:

* \(S_f(F,V)=H_f^1(F,V)\), finite/Bloch–Kato local conditions at every prime.
* \(S_{\rm can}(F,V)\), the same away from primes over p, but the **entire** H¹ at all primes over p.
* \(S_0(F,V)\), the same away from p, but the **zero** local condition at all primes over p.

At every p-adic local field and its Qp-linear restriction, the H_f local space is the local Kummer image, and the singular quotient H¹/H_f has dimension equal to the local degree for an elliptic curve. In particular over Qp both spaces are one-dimensional.

## Lemma 1: one-dimensional p-local image of rational Mordell–Weil

Since a non-torsion rational point has nonzero elliptic p-adic logarithm, the map
\[
E(Q)\otimes Q_p\longrightarrow H_f^1(Q_p,V)
\]
is onto. Its kernel has dimension one, generated by
\(\eta_p=\log_p(Q)P-\log_p(P)Q\) for any fixed MW basis P,Q.

By the global Kummer exact sequence,
\[
\dim S_f(Q,V)=2+t_p,\qquad
\dim S_0(Q,V)=1+t_p.
\]
The strict exact sequence is
\[
0\to Q_p\eta_p\to S_0(Q,V)\to
T_p\Sha(E/Q)\otimes Q_p\to0,
\]
since every global class's p-local finite image can be cancelled using a rational MW class.

For the rank-zero twist, Kolyvagin's theorem gives
\[
S_f(Q,V^-)=0,\qquad S_0(Q,V^-)=0.
\]

## Lemma 2: Poitou–Tate computes the relaxed Selmer space exactly

The self-duality \(V\simeq V^*(1)\), the orthogonality of finite local Kummer conditions, and the comparison between finite and relaxed conditions yield
\[
0\to S_f(Q,V)\to S_{\rm can}(Q,V)
 \to H_s^1(Q_p,V)\xrightarrow{\operatorname{loc}_{f,p}^\vee}
 S_f(Q,V)^\vee.
\]

The displayed connecting map is dual to the finite localization \(S_f(Q,V)\to H_f^1(Q_p,V)\). Lemma 1 makes that localization surjective; hence the connecting map is injective. It follows that
\[
\boxed{S_{\rm can}(Q,V)=S_f(Q,V),\quad
\dim S_{\rm can}(Q,V)=2+t_p.}
\]
For the twist, since \(S_f(Q,V^-)=0\), the same sequence gives
\[
\boxed{S_{\rm can}(Q,V^-)\simeq H_s^1(Q_p,V^-),\quad
\dim S_{\rm can}(Q,V^-)=1.}
\]
The twist's extra relaxed direction exists **unconditionally** over Qp, without postulating a derived Kato class.

## Theorem: all-p dimensions over K

Restriction/cores\-triction and the character idempotents \(e_\pm=(1\pm c)/2\) (p odd) give for **each** local condition \(\star\in\{f,0,\mathrm{can}\}\):
\[
S_\star(K,V)\simeq
S_\star(Q,V)\oplus S_\star(Q,V^-).
\]
At split p the local \(\pm\) eigenspaces are diagonal/anti-diagonal; at inert p they are the invariant/anti-invariant lines of the quadratic local extension. The finite, strict and relaxed conditions are preserved by these decompositions.

Combining Lemmas 1–2 gives for all good \(p\ge5\):
\[
\boxed{
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
& S_f&S_0&S_{\rm can}\\ \hline
E/Q &2+t_p&1+t_p&2+t_p\\
E^d/Q &0&0&1\\
E/K &2+t_p&1+t_p&3+t_p
\end{array}
}
\]
Entries are **Qp-dimensions**. Hence
\[
\dim S_{\rm can}(K,V)-\dim S_0(K,V)=2,
\]
but the canonical relaxed Selmer group has Qp-dimension at least **3**, not 2, even when Sha=0.

## Consequence for higher-rank Kolyvagin primitivity

A rank-two Kolyvagin/Stark system for the *unmodified canonical structure over K* with a primitivity theorem implying that its strict dual Selmer is Zp-torsion (hence zero after tensor Qp) is impossible, because \(S_0(K,V)\) always contains the geometric line Qp eta_p.

The exact target instead is
\[
S_0(K,V)/Q_p\eta_p \simeq
T_p\Sha(E/Q)\otimes Q_p.
\]
Nonvanishing of generalized Kato classes as in Castella–Hsieh, when applicable at a given p, forces t_p=0; it says this quotient vanishes, NOT that the strict Selmer is zero.

The existing rank-zero Euler system for the twist may furnish a genuinely anti-invariant class spanning the singular local direction in \(S_{\rm can}(Q,V^-)\). Even if it does, **the two MW directions and that class generate at most a 3-dimensional subspace**; showing no other dimensions requires the same p-finiteness input t_p=0. The ordinary Kato class for E at analytic rank 2 does not furnish a primitive additional rank-two system for free.

Burns–Kurihara–Sano's Kato derivatives over *real abelian* extensions of Q do not automatically yield norm-compatible classes over imaginary K. Neither the real-abelian theorem nor the rank-zero twist alone establishes global all-p integrality for the plus Selmer.

## Critical remaining global obligations

Even an independent proof of \(t_p=0\) for all p shows only that each p-primary Sha is **finite**, not that there is a finite set of p with nonzero finite parts. One still needs a fixed integer coming from a genuinely **uniform integral comparison** with actual Selmer Fitting ideals; the existing exact-profinite counterexample demonstrates why an arbitrary collection of p-local bounds is insufficient.

Even proving Sha=0 requires separately proving the archimedean equality \(L''(E,1)/2=\Omega_E\mathrm{Reg}(E)\). A numerical enclosure of the ratio near 1 does not force this exact equality without an integral/rationality theorem independent of BSD.
