## 4. Proof by local lattices

Two full-rank quaternionic Z-lattices are equal if and only if their completions at all rational primes agree.

- At p: 19 is a unit, so D_r(a)_p=L(a)_p. The quaternion g is a unit at p because Nrd(g)=19. In the split chart, left multiplication by g/19 acts diagonally with entries s0=(5+3r0)/19, s1=(5+3r1)/19, both p-adic units. We have an **exact** matrix factorization

  diag(s0,s1) * t_a
       = t_(w a) * diag(s0,s1),

  where w=s0/s1, and the right diagonal matrix lies in GL2(Z_p). Therefore the local right ideal is precisely T(w a)_p. The same reasoning gives w^-1 for gbar.

- At ell=19: p and m are 19-adic units, so T(a)_19 is the unchanged base right-order module, and L(a)_19 is its maximal left order. The split quadratic element q has two distinct 19-adic eigenvalues lifting residues 9,4. Write q=diag(q0,q1) locally, with q0≡9 mod19 and q1≡4 mod19. The left ideal D_9(a)_19 is, up to a suitable integral split-basis change, M2(Z_19) diag(19,1). The quaternion anti-involution corresponds to the matrix adjugate; hence conjugate(D_9(a)_19)=diag(1,19) M2(Z_19). Because g has norm 19 and vanishes modulo 19 in the first eigenspace, g/19 acts locally as diag(unit,19^-1*unit). Multiplication therefore cancels the two factors and recovers the original T(a)_19 exactly. The conjugate root4 follows with the two eigenspaces interchanged.

- At every other rational prime ell: D_r(a)_ell=L(a)_ell and g/19, gbar/19 are units in the embedded O_K⊗Z_ell, which sits inside L(a)_ell at ell!=p. Therefore all other local lattices are unchanged.

Equality at every completion proves both global ideal identities **for all p-adic unit parameters a**, with the orientation convention already used in the USER PASS case a=1.

The proof uses only ordinary local quaternion-order structure and invertibility, not a numerical heuristic or a frequency extrapolation.

## 5. Consequence and actual computational limitation

The same class g was user-certified to generate all H=87809710 ring classes of O_m, so one can generate the entire family of genuine Gross right ideals by deterministic oriented 19-neighbor steps. This is a mathematically exact recurrence, preserving the actual CM embedding at each stage.

But the state is **an oriented quaternionic ideal**, not just its Brandt class 0..32: the latter alone is insufficient to determine the next 19-neighbor. The universal identity does not eliminate the need to compute Brandt classes or weighted moments, nor does it constitute a fast H-step production algorithm. A practical accelerated implementation may need canonical reduction, cached ideal-isomorphisms, and controls on coefficient growth.
