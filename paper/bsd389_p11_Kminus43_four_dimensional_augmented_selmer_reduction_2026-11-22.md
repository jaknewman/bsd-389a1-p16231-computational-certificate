## 1. Arithmetic setting and a uniform sign obstruction

Let E/Q be `y²+y=x³+x²−2x`, N=389, sign w(E)=+1, algebraic and analytic rank two. For a **negative fundamental discriminant** D coprime to 389, write chi_D for the primitive quadratic character. The standard twist functional-equation formula is

    w(E^(D)) = w(E)*chi_D(-389) = -chi_D(389).

Therefore:

- If 389 **splits** in K=Q(sqrt(D)), chi_D(389)=+1, so the twist sign is -1, its central value vanishes, and the analytic rank of E/K is at least three. This universally prohibits any simultaneous choice of (i) Heegner splitting at 389 and (ii) a **rank-zero analytic twist** for this E. It does not prove the Mordell-Weil rank of the twist is positive without additional results.
- If 389 is **inert**, chi_D(389)=-1, so the twist sign is +1, allowing (but not guaranteeing) a rank-zero twist and an analytic-rank-two base change.

For D=-43: chi_{-43}(389)=-1, chi_{-43}(11)=+1. The project's exact rational interval proof gives `10 < L(E^(-43),1) < 11`, so by the established rank-zero theorem the twist has rank zero and finite Sha, and rank E(K)=2. For comparison, D=-7 splits at both 389 and 11 but has twist sign -1, hence cannot have a rank-zero twist. Both local computations are checked by the companion pure-Python script.

This shows why the **definite quaternion N^-=389** case is structurally appropriate for a rank-two imaginary quadratic base change. Macias Castillo–Sano's separate Heegner-splitting application cannot be imported unchanged into this rank-two setup. Their general Selmer-complex formalism remains mathematically relevant.

## 3. Four-dimensional unipotent Selmer theorem

**Theorem (conditional on the theta-unit hypothesis of section 2).** The actual module X_infty is free of rank FOUR over Z_11. In an integral basis, multiplication by T is a 4x4 matrix A over Z_11 satisfying A^4=0. Furthermore,

    4 - rank_F11(A mod 11)
      = 2 + dim_F11 Sha(E/Q)[11] + dim_F11 Sha(E^(-43)/Q)[11].           (*)

In particular,

    rank_F11(A mod 11)=2
         <=> Sha(E/K)[11^infinity]=0
         ==> Sha(E/Q)[11^infinity]=0.

The conclusion that the original E has trivial 11-primary Sha is unconditional **once** both a genuine theta unit for K=-43 and an authenticated rank-two mod-11 action matrix are established. Conversely, if the original Sha vanishes but the twist Sha has 11-torsion, the rank-two matrix criterion fails; it is a test for **both** components at once.

**Proof.** Since the characteristic ideal is theta times its involution, and theta=T²U with U a unit, `char_Lambda(X_infty)=(T^4)`. Because mu=0, the finitely generated torsion Lambda-module X_infty is finite over Z_11 up to a finite error, and by Corollary 4.2 it has no finite Lambda-submodule. Thus it is a free Z_11-module. Its rank over Z_11 equals the Weierstrass degree of its characteristic power series, namely four. Multiplication by T on the four-dimensional Q_11-space has characteristic polynomial T^4, so Cayley–Hamilton yields A^4=0 exactly on the Z_11-lattice.

Taking coinvariants and reducing modulo 11 gives

    (X_0/11 X_0) ~= (F_11)^4 / (A mod 11)(F_11)^4.

Its dimension equals 4−rank_F11(A mod11). The Kummer sequence and rank E(K)=2 show

    dim_F11 Sel(K,E[11]) = 2 + dim_F11 Sha(E/K)[11]

(the rational 11-torsion over K is zero since GL2(F11) remains absolutely irreducible on the index-two subgroup). For p odd, restriction/corestriction and the `+/-` idempotents split Sha(E/K)[11] into the original and the quadratic twist components. Pontryagin duality identifies the first dimension with dim(X_0/11 X_0). This proves (*).

If rank(A mod11)=2, the right side of (*) is 2, hence Sha(E/K)[11]=0. Any nonzero 11-primary torsion group has nonzero 11-torsion, so Sha(E/K)[11^infinity]=0, and therefore Sha(E/Q)[11^infinity]=0. Conversely, if Sha(E/K)[11^infinity]=0, equality (*) forces rank=2. QED.

## 4. Why this is more precise than a theta leading coefficient

For any integer e>=0, the previous paper's abstract module with `theta=T²`, `Fitt0=(T^4)` and action matrix

    A_e = [[0,-11^e,0,0],[0,0,0,0],[0,0,0,-11^e],[0,0,0,0]]

has the same characteristic theta square, and A_e^2=0 for every e. Yet `rank_F11(A_0)=2`, while `rank_F11(A_e)=0` for e>=1. The augmentation torsion has order `11^(2e)`. Consequently the genuinely **new** datum needed for Sha is the **mod-11 rank of the actual gamma action**, not more arithmetic on the theta polynomial alone. The self-test confirms all these statements, and also a different nilpotent Jordan type (3+1) with rank two.

## 6. Stronger finite-layer reformulation: no infinite-level matrix is necessary

Even *without* assuming a theta quadratic unit, Kim's Theorem 4.1, Corollary 4.2 and Proposition 4.3 imply unconditionally for our p=11,K=-43 conditions that X_infty is a **finite-rank free Z_11-module**, say of rank lambda, and that its augmentation is X_0. Consequently:

    dim_F11 Sha(E/K)[11]
      = lambda - rank_F11((gamma-1) | (X_infty/11X_infty)) - 2.

This is an exact finite-dimensional test, but lambda and the genuine action remain unknown. It does not assume Sha finite.

Under the additional genuine-theta-unit hypothesis, lambda=4. Reduce the first-layer relation `omega_1=(1+T)^11-1` modulo 11: it becomes `T^11`. Since the action A is nilpotent with A^4=0, this relation is automatically zero on X_infty/11. Thus Kim's **exact** control theorem gives

    (Sel(K_1,E[11^infinity])^vee)/11
          ~= (X_infty/11) / T^11(X_infty/11)
          ~= X_infty/11 ~= F_11^4.

The Galois group Gamma_1=Gal(K_1/K) acts in a way compatible with gamma, so **it suffices to determine the F_11[Gamma_1]-module Sel(K_1,E[11])** (or its dual). Its dimension must be 4, and

    rank_F11(gamma-1 | Sel(K_1,E[11]))=2
       <=> Sha(E/K)[11^infinity]=0
       ==> Sha(E/Q)[11^infinity]=0.

There is no hidden assumption that E(K_1)[11] is nonzero: indeed GL_2(F_11) surjectivity over Q implies any nonzero 11-torsion point has orbit of size 11^2−1=120; since `[K_1:Q]=22`, such a point cannot be defined over K_1. Thus the standard finite and p-infinity Selmer comparison at 11 applies.

**Caveat:** the first-layer Gross class-group representation, despite also being a Gal(K_1/K)-module, is not automatically the finite-layer Selmer module. The identification of their Fitting *ideals* is not an isomorphism of these modules.

This replaces an inaccessible infinite-action computation by a **finite 11-Selmer computation over a degree-22 number field**, conditional on the genuine K=-43 theta quadratic unit. Such a computation is nontrivial and has **not been performed**.
