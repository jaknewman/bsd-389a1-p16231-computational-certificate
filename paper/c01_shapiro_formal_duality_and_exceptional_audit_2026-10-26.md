## 1. Formal transport representation (not calibrated numerically)

Let p=16231, N=389, and h=[a,b;c,d] in Gamma_0(Np), hence c=0 mod p and ad=1 mod p. Define q(h)=-ab and r(h)=a² in Fp. The plus modular-symbol moments form a column v(D)=(M0(D),M1(D)) with

`M0(hD)=M0(D), M1(hD)=q(h)M0(D)+r(h)M1(D)`.

Thus v(hD)=T(h)v(D), `T(h)=[[1,0],[q(h),r(h)]]`. For any h,h' in Gamma_0(Np), direct multiplication of their upper-triangular reductions mod p yields

`q(hh')=q(h)+r(h)q(h'), r(hh')=r(h)r(h')`,

so `T(hh')=T(h)T(h')`. This is an exact finite-field representation, with no input from V19 or any arbitrary pair tests.

On the dual row `(C0,C1)`, the action is `(C0,C1)T(h)=(C0+q(h)C1, r(h)C1)`. The initial C01 row for a fragment with weight w and Teich correction kappa is `(-(-w*kappa),-w)` after setting `W=-w, K=-w*kappa`: `(C0,C1)=(-K,W)=(w*kappa,-w)`. Multiplying by `T(h)` yields `(q(h)W-K,r(h)W)`. Contracting against the basis v(D) gives `W M1(hD)-K M0(hD)=-w[M1(hD)-kappa M0(hD)]`. Hence the C01 pushforward and independently implemented direct scalar pullback agree **for arbitrary** (M0,M1) whenever h lies in Gamma_0(Np).

## 2. Relation branch and the K-term invariant

For a non-generator representative R_i, the independently exactly verified Manin cusp-divisor relation is `F(R_i)=sum_t e_t F(A_t^-1 R_{g_t})`, with e_t in {+1,-1}, A_t in Gamma_0(N). Left-multiply by Shapiro representative r_j and split `r_j A_t^-1=h_t r_{k_t}`, h_t in Gamma_0(Np). Consequently

`M0(r_j R_i)=sum_t e_t M0(r_{k_t} R_{g_t})`;

`M1(r_j R_i)=sum_t e_t[q_t M0(r_{k_t}R_{g_t})+r_t M1(r_{k_t}R_{g_t})]`.

Applying the external initial h then produces, term by term, dual coefficients

`C0_t=e_t*[(q(h)+r(h)q_t)W-K]`,

`C1_t=e_t*r(h)*r_t*W`.

**Critical invariant:** `-K` is multiplied by e_t, but *not* by r(h). Moving `-K` inside the q-composition or multiplying it by r(h) would be mathematically wrong. The archived V11 implementation and the full second scalar's relation pullback have this same structure, as seen directly in their source.

The lower left entry of `H*r_infty^-1` is `N*(p*c-d)` and is divisible by Np because d=0 mod p. This proves the same Γ0(Np) decomposition for j=infinity. The finite-field transport formula applies unchanged.
