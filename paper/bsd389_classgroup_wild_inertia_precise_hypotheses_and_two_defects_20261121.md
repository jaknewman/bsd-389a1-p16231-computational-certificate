## 1. What the PRIMARY SOURCE actually assumes

D. Prasad and S. Shekhar, *Relating Tate-Shafarevich group of an
elliptic curve with class group*, arXiv:1912.12928v1 (2019),
**Theorem 4.1** explicitly assumes:
(a) good reduction at p;
(b) **if E is ordinary and a_p(E)!=1 (mod p), E[p] is wildly
ramified at p**;
(c) p-unit Tamagawa factors outside p;
(d) E[p] is irreducible.
Their proof (last paragraph of Theorem 4.1) states that if the
exceptional bad-prime set T is empty then the G-equivariant
unramified class-group quotient equals the *everywhere-unramified
cohomology* group R_p(E/Q), as defined in their section 4.
Full source:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.12928, printed pp. 9--11.

Ray--Weston, *Class group statistics for torsion fields generated by
elliptic curves*, arXiv:2204.09757v2, Theorems 2.1, 2.3:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2204.09757
reports the stronger published Prasad--Shekhar bound
  d_p <= rank E(Q)+dim_Fp Sha[p]-1+#I
under its conditions, whereas the older arXiv Theorem 4.1
prints d_p<=dim Sel_p+#T. **These are not the same
bound or the same stated local hypotheses.** Do not silently
apply the stronger version outside the precise regime verified
in a published-source audit.

The 2026-11-21 predecessor NOTE omitted the wild-ramification
hypothesis when claiming an *unconditional* isomorphism
  C_p = S_p^str
at every good nonanomalous p. That overstatement should
not be used as an input in any BSD proof.

## 2. Safe, completely unconditional comparison for this curve

Let p>=5, p not in {97,389}, with full image G=GL2(Fp),
and assume p is nonanomalous, i.e. p does not divide
#E(Fp). Put V=E[p], K=Q(E[p]),

 S_p=Sel(Q,E[p]),
 S_p^str=ker(S_p->H^1(Q_p,V)),
 C_p=Hom_G(Cl(K)/p,V),
 s_p=dim_Fp Sha(E/Q)[p],
 epsilon_p=dim_Fp image(S_p -> H^1(Q_p,V)) in {0,1},
 d_p=dim_Fp C_p.

The **restriction map ALWAYS injects**
 S_p^str -> C_p:
- Sah's lemma and full residual image give H^1(G,V)=0;
- all good ell!=p Kummer localizations are unramified;
- p-strict classes localize to zero at p;
- at ell=389 split I_1 and p∤388 ensure
  E(Q_389)/pE(Q_389)=0, hence localization zero.
Global class field theory identifies the unramified
cohomology over K with C_p.

Consequently, without ANY claim about the opposite direction,
 d_p >= dim S_p^str = 2+s_p-epsilon_p,
with epsilon_p in {0,1}.
For the even p-Selmer parity, if Sha[p]!=0 then s_p>=2,
so d_p>=3, yielding p^6|h(K) and, by the previously
proved tame covector descent, p^3|h(F) where
 F=K^{diag(1,a)} has degree p(p^2-1).

This is a rigorous UNIFORM NECESSARY condition but NO
uniform upper bound. The cases p=97 and anomalous p
require their earlier separately formulated conditions.

## 3. An exact two-defect identity (no hidden surjectivity assumption)

Define an *additional unramified-class defect*
  eta_p := dim_Fp C_p - dim_Fp S_p^str >= 0.
It measures how many G-equivariant everywhere-unramified
classes of K are not accounted for by the p-strict
elliptic Kummer Selmer group. The definition does not
presuppose finite Sha.

Then, EXACTLY:
  **d_p = 2 + s_p - epsilon_p + eta_p**.

When the Kim + Castella--Sano hypotheses and published
theorems are applied to E389a1, their established
unit-Kurihara minimal degree mu_p is
  mu_p=2+s_p.
Thus the rigorous bridge is
  **mu_p = d_p + epsilon_p - eta_p**.

One may write mu_p=2 ceil(d_p/2) ONLY AFTER
proving the needed extra conditions on eta_p and
epsilon_p, not merely from parity and class-field
injection. It is not an unconditional formula for
all good nonanomalous p.

## 4. Conditional exactness under the SOURCE'S wild-inertia regime

Suppose in addition to Section 2 that:
- E[p] is wildly ramified at p if p is ordinary
  (for supersingular p this clause is vacuous);
- the precise Prasad--Shekhar source hypotheses apply.

Then the original arXiv Theorem 4.1 proof with T=empty
identifies C_p with their everywhere-unramified
group R_p(Q,V). At nonanomalous good p the unramified
local H^1(Q_p,V) is zero; at 389 it is also zero
because Frob acts by 389!=1 on the inertia-invariant

elliptic Kummer. Thus R_p=S_p^str and eta_p=0,
whence d_p=2+s_p-epsilon_p.

If one ALSO applies the distinct, stronger
published Prasad--Shekhar upper bound as stated by
Ray--Weston Theorem 2.3 with I=empty, then
  d_p<=1+s_p.
Comparing with the lower bound gives epsilon_p=1
and d_p=1+s_p, so under BOTH theorem statements
and verified hypotheses, the global rank-two MW
localization is primitive. This cannot be asserted
for all ordinary nonanomalous primes until the
wild-inertia / published-version hypotheses have
been independently verified across that set.

The arXiv Theorem 4.1 alone provides the *weaker*
upper d_p<=2+s_p, and does NOT imply epsilon_p=1.
One cannot deduce a universal local primitivity

## 5. Exact finite arithmetic demonstrates the pointwise hazard

For E389a1 with P=(-1,1), Q=(0,-1), rational point
addition modulo p^2 gives:
  p=223: a_p=-5, #E(Fp)=229,
    t([229]P)/p=0 mod223, t([229]Q)/p=94 mod223;
  p=419: a_p=-35, #E(Fp)=455,
