# BSD 389a1 — a fully uniform Kummer–Chebotarev two-point separation theorem (2026-11-08)

**Status:** New unconditional mathematical consequence of known theorems for the fixed curve, valid **for every p>=5**, including ordinary, supersingular, and the bad prime 389. Explicit small-p tests independently executed. Crucially this is about **local Kummer images of rational Mordell–Weil points**, NOT the modular-symbol Kurihara numbers. It does **not** prove Sha(E)[p∞]=0 or rank-two complex BSD.

## 0. Inputs and notation

E/Q: y²+y=x³+x²−2x, minimal discriminant +389 and conductor 389. The previously certified complete saturated Mordell–Weil group is Z·P ⊕ Z·Q with P=(-1,1) and Q=(0,-1), torsion zero.

For p>=11, mod-p surjectivity follows from Mazur's theorem for semistable E/Q; p=5,7 were independently certified in the archive. LMFDB 389.a1 also reports maximal ell-adic image at every ell.

Put V=E[p], L=Q(E[p]) and M=L(p^{-1}P,p^{-1}Q), the field generated by **all** p-division preimages of the two points.

## 1. Full Kummer extension theorem, simultaneously for every p>=5

**Theorem:** Gal(M/L)=V⊕V as an Fp[GL2(Fp)]-module, and |Gal(M/Q)|=|GL2(Fp)| p^4.

**Proof.** Since rho_{E,p} is surjective, G=Gal(L/Q)=GL2(Fp). The central element -I acts as -1 on V and its difference from identity -2 is invertible (p odd). Sah's lemma therefore yields H^1(G,V)=0. The inflation-restriction sequence injects H^1(Q,V) into H^1(L,V)^G. Because P,Q form a saturated Z-basis of E(Q), their images under Kummer are linearly independent in E(Q)/p ↪ H^1(Q,V), hence remain independent in Hom(G_L,V).

The combined map G_L -> V² given by the two Kummer cocycles has image W, a G-stable submodule of V². The natural V is irreducible with End_G(V)=Fp. Because V² is semisimple (a direct sum of two simple modules), any proper submodule W is annihilated by a nonzero G-equivariant map V²→V, necessarily (v,w)↦a v+b w with (a,b)≠0. This would make the corresponding linear combination of the restricted Kummer cocycles zero, contradicting independence. Thus W=V². QED.

**Caution:** This is a theorem of Kummer extension maximality for two *known rational points*. It has no Selmer rank upper bound.

## 2. Auxiliary primes with an invertible rank-two local Kummer determinant

**Theorem:** For each p>=5, there are infinitely many distinct primes q1 and q2 (q_i∤389p) such that
- q_i≡1 (mod p), a_{q_i}≡2≡q_i+1 (mod p);
- Frobenius at q_i in GL2(Fp) is **nontrivial unipotent**, so dim E(F_q_i)[p]=1;
- P is NOT p-divisible in E(F_q1) but Q is; P is p-divisible in E(F_q2) but Q is NOT.

**Proof.** Let g be a nonidentity unipotent 2×2 matrix. Then ker(g−1) and V/(g−1)V each have dimension 1. Because Gal(M/L)=V², for any chosen lift of g to Gal(M/Q), multiplication by Gal(M/L) permits prescribing the two translation coordinates arbitrarily in V². In particular we can prescribe nonzero/zero classes in [V/(g−1)V]² as (1,0) and (0,1). The two prescribed Frobenius subsets are stable under conjugation; Chebotarev gives infinitely many unramified rational primes with each behavior. The finite-field Kummer isomorphism E(F_q)/p ≅ H^1(F_q,E[p]) ≅ V/(g−1)V identifies zero translation with p-divisibility. Finally det g=1 and tr g=2 prove the auxiliary congruences. QED.

For a fixed nonidentity unipotent g, among the p^4 possible translation pairs, exactly p(p−1) coordinates v project nonzero to V/(g−1)V while p coordinates w project to zero. Thus there are p²(p−1) lifts with pattern (1,0). There are p²−1 nonidentity unipotent matrices. Consequently the exact Chebotarev density of **each** pattern is

    [(p²−1) p²(p−1)] / [ |GL2(Fp)| p⁴ ] = 1/p³.

This is a stronger uniform result than mere existence of admissible q, which has density p/[(p−1)²(p+1)].

## 4. Precisely what Kim does and does not establish uniformly

Chan-Ho Kim, with Pollack appendix, arXiv:2505.09121v1, Theorems 1.1 and 1.2 and Corollary 1.5:

- Theorem 1.2 (ord) guarantees **some** nonzero minimally normalized Kurihara number δ̃_n for every good ordinary p>=5 satisfying large image and p-distinguished; the latter is automatic for a good ordinary elliptic curve at p>=5.
- Theorem 1.1 identifies the discrete Selmer corank with ord(δ̃)=the minimum number ν(n) of prime factors for which δ̃_n is nonzero.
- For this E, δ̃_1=0 because L(E,1)=0 and functional-equation symmetry kills odd ν(n). Hence ord(δ̃)∈{2,4,6,...} for each such ordinary prime.
- This **does not imply ord(δ̃)=2**. Establishing a two-prime *unit* witness remains necessary to prove that Selmer corank is 2 and Sha[p∞] finite. A unit two-prime witness additionally forces the finite Sha group to vanish.
- For supersingular p, Theorem 1.2(ord) does not apply; Theorem 1.1 still applies if a nonzero δ̃_n is found and its large-image hypothesis holds.

There is NO established identity of the form
  δ̃_{q1q2} (mod p) = (p-adic unit) × det(local Kummer images of P,Q)
uniformly in p. Asserting one is exactly the missing arithmetic reciprocity comparison; it must not be silently assumed.

Our new theorem gives a **unit local MW determinant** for each p, but this says nothing yet about the Kato Euler system and the extra possible Tate–Shafarevich classes in the actual p-Selmer group. It does not imply Sha finiteness or the complex BSD coefficient.

## 5. Independent real leading coefficient gate remains

The previously proved exact Mellin–Fricke series and Faltings–Hriljac modular degree 40 height identity give L''(E,1)/2 and Ω_E Reg(E) as two separately exact real expressions. Their equality still requires an independent second-complex-derivative height reciprocity theorem or a proven rationality/denominator bound for their ratio; no implication from the Kummer separation theorem to that equality is known.
