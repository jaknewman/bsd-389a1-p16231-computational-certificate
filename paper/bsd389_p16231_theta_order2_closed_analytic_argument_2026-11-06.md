### Step 3. Lift nonvanishing to the infinite series

The published Appendix §6 states
Theta_{f/K}(T) congruent Theta_{f/K,1}(T)
  modulo ((1+T)^p-1).
Modulo p this ideal becomes (T^p), by the characteristic-p binomial theorem. Hence the coefficient of T^2 in the actual infinite theta is a p-adic unit. Note that vanishing *modulo p* of the constant and linear coefficients is insufficient by itself for exact order two.

### Step 4. Exact vanishing of the lower coefficients

First, L(E,1)=0 exactly. This follows from its established Mordell--Weil rank 2 and the modular elliptic-curve rank-zero theorem: if L(E,1) were nonzero, its algebraic rank would be zero. Hence L(E/K,1)=L(E,1)*L(E^(-3),1)=0.

By Castella--Hsieh Theorem 2.3 for the trivial ring-class character, applicable since gcd(p*389,3)=1 and N^-=389 is squarefree with one inert factor, the square of Theta_{f/K}(0) is a specified constant times L(E/K,1). Thus Theta_{f/K}(0)=0 exactly.

Second, Chida--Hsieh's functional equation (Theorem B in their paper, or Theorem 4.8 in the long version) is
Theta^*=epsilon_p(f)*epsilon(f)*Theta*sigma_(Nplus)^(-1)
in the trivial tame branch. Here Nplus=1, since the only conductor prime 389 is inert in K; epsilon_p(f)=+1 at the good prime p; epsilon(f)=+1 by the exact Sage root-number check. Therefore Theta^*=Theta.

In the coordinate 1+T=gamma, the involution is T -> -T/(1+T). Write Theta(T)=a0+a1*T+a2*T^2+... . Comparing coefficients of T gives -a1=a1, hence a1=0 EXACTLY over Z_p.

Since a0=a1=0 exactly and a2 is nonzero modp, the order of vanishing in T is precisely two.

## What this does NOT prove

This identifies an infinite anticyclotomic theta order, not the exact order of Sha or full BSD. To obtain finiteness of the 16231-primary Sha, one must invoke Castella--Hsieh Theorems B and A with an auxiliary weight-one CM form g such that L(E^K,1)*L(E/K,chi,1)!=0, rho_E,p irreducible and ramified at N-=389, and distinct p-stabilization roots. The authors' Appendix §6 invokes Vatsal/Chida--Hsieh existence of suitable ring-class characters under the field hypotheses, but this theorem's exact applicability should be separately documented before promoting the Selmer corollary to unconditional status.

The nonvanishing of the twist L(E^(-3),1) has a separately run exact rational interval certificate (4.51,4.60). There is presently no claim that Sha[p^infinity]=0; its finiteness is strictly weaker.
