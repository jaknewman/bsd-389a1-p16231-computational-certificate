## Theorem 1: a single EXACT Fitt2 identity for every odd p — no anticyclotomic control needed

For EVERY odd rational prime p, including p=3 and the bad conductor prime p=389, put
\[
X_{K,p}=\operatorname{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/K)^\vee,\quad
I_{+,p}=\operatorname{Fitt}_{0,Z_p}(\Sha(E/Q)[p^\infty]^\vee),\quad
I_{-,p}=\operatorname{Fitt}_{0,Z_p}(\Sha(E^d/Q)[p^\infty]^\vee).
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Fitt}_{2,Z_p}(X_{K,p})=I_{+,p}I_{-,p}.}\tag{1}
\]

**Proof:** The Kummer exact sequence and E(K) free of rank exactly 2 yield
\(X_{K,p}\simeq Z_p^2\oplus\Sha(E/K)[p^\infty]^\vee\) as Zp-modules, by projectivity of Zp². For odd p, the quadratic idempotents and restriction/corestriction identify \(\Sha(E/K)[p^\infty]\simeq \Sha(E/Q)[p^\infty]\oplus\Sha(E^d/Q)[p^\infty]\). Higher Fitting shifts by the free summand and Fitt0 of a direct sum is the product. These statements do not presuppose any Sha finiteness. QED.

Let \(D_-=\#\Sha(E^d/Q)\), a single **proved-to-exist but not numerically evaluated finite** integer. Then for every odd p not dividing D_-,
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Fitt}_{2,Z_p}(X_{K,p})
=\operatorname{Fitt}_{2,Z_p}(\operatorname{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/Q)^\vee).}\tag{2}
\]
This is an exact p-uniform equality for **ALL** such primes; the excluded prime set is the finite support of an ACTUALLY proved finite group (the twist), not a hypothetical E-Sha exceptional set.

## Theorem 2: a single UNIFORM basechange Fitting determinant suffices for all odd Sha of E

Suppose an independent global theorem constructs a concrete integer \(D\ne0\), not defined using any Sha group, and proves
\[
D Z_p\subseteq\operatorname{Fitt}_{2,Z_p}(X_{K,p})
\qquad\text{for EVERY odd }p.
\]
Then the entire ODD-primary Sha(E/Q) is finite, its order divides the odd part of D, and p-primary groups vanish for p not dividing D.

**Proof:** By (1), \(\operatorname{Fitt}_{2}(X_{K,p})=I_{+,p}I_{-,p}\subseteq I_{+,p}\), so the inclusion for the K-Selmer implies \(D Z_p\subseteq I_{+,p}\). For a finite p-group, Fitt0 of its Pontryagin dual is generated by its order; if the dual has positive free rank, its Fitt0 is zero and cannot contain nonzero D. Thus every odd-primary part of Sha(E/Q) is finite with \(\#\Sha_p\mid p^{v_p(D)}\). Only the finitely many p dividing D can occur. QED.

If D is squarefree, the order-square theorem of Cassels–Tate kills all odd parts automatically, and a separately accepted 2-primary certificate would settle ALL of Sha.

**Critical limitation:** Merely POSTULATING D in the theorem is equivalent to the sought all-prime finiteness statement (diagonal Fitting intersection), so this is a VALID SUFFICIENT CRITERION, not a construction of D. One must produce D from an independent integral motivic/Euler-system/derived-regulator object with genuine uniform comparison. Finite Gross toric norm 729, Hecke Smith index 2, or one prime's theta derivative are **not** proved choices of D.

By Artin formalism (at the identity of actual L-functions), for all s
\(L(E/K,s)=L(E,s)L(E^d,s)\).
The exact analytic rank of E is two and L(E^d,1)>0, hence
\[
\boxed{\frac{L''(E/K,1)}2
=L(E^d,1)\frac{L''(E,1)}2.}\tag{3}
\]

The full MW group and the absolute Néron–Tate height regulator agree EXACTLY over Q and K; there is no hidden index. This means a genuine proven rank-two real regulator formula for basechange E/K could be transported to Q using only explicit periods, component groups and the separately rank-zero twist's BSD factor.
