# BSD 389a1: an exact rank-two Kurihara–Selmer dictionary for ALL good primes p >= 5 (8 Nov 2026)

**Status:** A rigorous *deduction* from the statements of Kim (arXiv:2505.09121, Theorem 1.1) and Castella–Sano (arXiv:2601.14504, Theorem B); the latter is a January 2026 preprint. This does **not** prove all-prime Sha vanishing nor the rank-two complex BSD identity. No old p-primary or complex certificates have been rerun.

## 1. Valid range

Fix E/Q: y²+y=x³+x²−2x, N=389, Delta_min=+389, Tamagawa product 1, torsion 0, and proven full saturated MW rank 2 generated by P=(-1,1), Q=(0,-1). The analytic rank is 2 and global root number is +1.

Let p>=5 be any prime **p != 389**, so E has good reduction. Large-image/surjective residual representation for these p is from Mazur (p>=11 for semistable E) plus archived independent p=5,7 certificates; LMFDB reports maximal ell-adic images. The optimal 389-level modular parametrization has p-integral Manin normalization because p² does not divide N, cf. Mazur's result cited explicitly in Castella–Sano p.2. These are precisely the input hypotheses of the two source theorems in their elliptic specialization.

Let δ_n be the integrally Néron-normalized Kurihara modular symbol of conductor n (squarefree product of eligible primes), as in Castella–Sano 2026 §1.1. It lives in Z/I_n, not in a fixed Fp for every n; write bar δ_n for reduction modulo p. Define
- rho_p = min { ν(n): δ_n ≠ 0 in Z/I_n };
- M_r = min over ν(n)=r of the p-divisibility index M(n), with M(n)=infinity for δ_n=0;
- M_infty = lim_{r parity +} M_r.

## 2. NEW unconditional-to-source THEOREM (not a new proof of the sources)

Castella–Sano Theorem B proves for p good ordinary, or good supersingular with squarefree N, that M_infty = v_p(Tam_E) = 0. Since N=389 is prime and every good p>=5 is ordinary or supersingular, for **every p>=5, p≠389**:

    M_infty = 0,  and some bar δ_n != 0.

This is genuine all-good-prime *some-length* Kurihara nonvanishing. It extends the previous ordinary-only information to infinitely many supersingular primes.

Moreover, the functional equation sign + and L(E,1)=0 imply δ_1=0 and δ_n=0 for odd ν(n). Thus rho_p is an even integer at least 2.

Kim Theorem 1.1 (under large image and nonzero collection) gives

    corank_Zp Sel_{p∞}(E/Q) = rho_p,
    length_Zp[ Sel_{p∞}(E/Q) / (Sel_{p∞}(E/Q))_div ]
        = M_{rho_p} - M_infty = M_{rho_p}.

The Kummer sequence and MW rank 2 split as cofinitely generated Zp-modules:

    Sel_{p∞}(E/Q) ≅ (Qp/Zp)^2 ⊕ Sha(E/Q)[p∞].

Consequently we have the EXACT rank-two arithmetic dictionary

    corank_Zp Sha(E/Q)[p∞] = rho_p - 2,

and the finite quotient of Sha by its maximal divisible subgroup has order p^{M_{rho_p}}.

In particular:

    rho_p = 2  <==>  Sha(E/Q)[p∞] is FINITE,
    and in that case v_p(#Sha(E/Q)[p∞]) = M_2.

Moreover,

    [exists eligible q1 != q2 with bar δ_(q1q2) != 0]
      <==> [rho_p = 2 and M_2=0]
      <==> [Sha(E/Q)[p∞] = 0].

Proof of final equivalence: a unit two-prime number forces rho=2 and M2=0 by definition, then Kim+Castella–Sano gives Sha_p=0. Conversely if Sha_p=0 then the Selmer corank is exactly 2 and finite quotient is zero; Kim gives rho=2 and M2=M_infty=0. The last equality means a two-prime unit witness exists.

**What this means:** proving the two-prime unit witness at all sufficiently large p would solve exactly the missing all-prime Sha problem. This criterion is **not** a separate proof that the witnesses exist.

## 3. The local Kummer determinant is NOT a substitute for the global theorem

Earlier in this PR we proved by Kummer–Chebotarev that for every p>=5 there exist infinitely many pairs of eligible q1,q2 whose two MW point reductions give an invertible 2×2 matrix mod p. This detects the known rank-2 rational Mordell–Weil subspace.

The scalar δ_(q1q2), by contrast, is a **derived global modular-symbol / Kato Euler-system value**. A formula

    bar δ_(q1q2) = u_(p,q1,q2) det( local_Kummer(P,Q) ),
    u_(p,q1,q2) in Fp^×,

would be a NEW integral reciprocity theorem. None of the papers above proves this formula or that its comparison scalar is always a unit.

Indeed, the exact Selmer structure theorem shows why this is a substantive obstacle: the local determinant remains nonzero on a two-dimensional MW subspace even if extra p-Selmer/Sha directions exist. The missing comparison must detect/control those global directions. A *purely local* inference would simply assume away the obstruction.

## 5. Remaining distinct gates

* **Arithmetic:** Find a noncircular theorem forcing rho_p=2 and M2=0 for all sufficiently large good p, or a global Selmer annihilator. The Castella–Sano M_infty=0 theorem does NOT force rho_p=2. p=389 is bad and outside their explicit unconditional Theorem B; small 2,3 need separate sources.
* **Complex:** Independently prove L''(E,1)/2 = Omega_E Reg_E (expected Sha=1), by an exact rank-two archimedean height reciprocity or independent rationality/denominator theorem. None of Kim, Castella–Sano, Kummer–Chebotarev or the existing Faltings–Hriljac divisor normalization provides this identity.
