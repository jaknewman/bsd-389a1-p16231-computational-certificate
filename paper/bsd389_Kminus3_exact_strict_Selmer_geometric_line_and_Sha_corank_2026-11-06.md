# The exact strict-Selmer defect over K=Q(sqrt(-3)): a one-dimensional geometric line plus Sha

Date: 2026-11-06. **Exact uniform cohomological theorem at every good odd p**, not a local experiment, not a proof of Sha finiteness. This improves and corrects the preceding quadratic rank-two Euler-system proposal.

## Assumptions previously proved for E=389a1

- \(E(\mathbf Q)\cong\mathbf ZP\oplus\mathbf ZQ\), torsion 0.
- \(K=\mathbf Q(\sqrt{-3})\); the twist \(E^d=E^{(-3)}\) has analytic rank 0 by a certified positive central L-value, hence algebraic rank 0 and finite \(\Sha(E^d/\mathbf Q)\) by the standard analytic-rank-zero theorem.
- For an **arbitrary** odd prime p of good reduction, excluding p=3 to avoid irrelevant ramification bookkeeping, put \(V=V_p E\), and use the usual Bloch–Kato finite conditions at every place.

Define
\[
S_f(K,V)=H_f^1(K,V),\qquad
S_{0,p}(K,V)=\ker\left(S_f(K,V)\xrightarrow{\mathrm{loc}_p}
\bigoplus_{v\mid p}H_f^1(K_v,V)\right).
\]
Let \(A_p=\Sha(E/\mathbf Q)[p^\infty]\), a discrete cofinitely generated p-primary group, and put \(W_p=T_p A_p\otimes_{\mathbf Z_p}\mathbf Q_p\). Its dimension is precisely the \(\mathbf Z_p\)-corank of \(A_p\). This remains meaningful if \(A_p\) is infinite.

For non-torsion rational \(P,Q\), let
\(\ell_{P,p}=\log_{E,p}(P)\in\mathbf Q_p\) and
\(\ell_{Q,p}=\log_{E,p}(Q)\in\mathbf Q_p\),
using the same nonzero invariant differential. Define
\[
\eta_p=\ell_{Q,p}\,P-\ell_{P,p}\,Q
\in E(\mathbf Q)\otimes\mathbf Q_p.
\]
The logarithm extends from the open formal subgroup to \(E(\mathbf Q_p)\otimes\mathbf Q_p\). It is nonzero on non-torsion points; both \(\ell_{P,p},\ell_{Q,p}\) are nonzero. Therefore \(\eta_p\ne0\).

## Theorem (uniform in p, not assuming any Sha finiteness)

For every odd good prime \(p\ne3\) there is a natural exact sequence of \(\mathbf Q_p\)-vector spaces
\[
\boxed{
0\longrightarrow \mathbf Q_p\eta_p
\longrightarrow S_{0,p}(K,V)
\longrightarrow W_p
\longrightarrow0.
}\tag{*}
\]

Consequently
\[
\boxed{
\dim_{\mathbf Q_p}S_{0,p}(K,V)
=1+\operatorname{corank}_{\mathbf Z_p}\Sha(E/\mathbf Q)[p^\infty].
}\tag{**}
\]

In particular the *correct* p-finiteness criterion is
\[
\boxed{
\Sha(E/\mathbf Q)[p^\infty]\text{ finite}
\iff
S_{0,p}(K,V)=\mathbf Q_p\eta_p.
}\tag{***}
\]
Finite \(\Sha[p^\infty]\) need NOT be zero: integral Fitting/leading-term data are still necessary.

### Proof

**1. Rational +/- decomposition.** For p odd, the idempotents \(e_\pm=(1\pm c)/2\), where \(c\in\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbf Q)\), split Galois cohomology and preserve Bloch–Kato local conditions. Hence
\[
S_f(K,V)=S_f(\mathbf Q,V)\oplus
S_f(\mathbf Q,V\otimes\chi_{-3}).
\]
The second term is ZERO: the Mordell–Weil group of \(E^{-3}\) has rank zero and its \(\Sha\) is finite, so its rational p-adic Selmer group vanishes. Thus all classes of \(S_f(K,V)\) are plus-invariant and identify with the original Selmer over Q.

**2. Kummer quotient.** Taking rational Tate modules of the Kummer exact sequence gives
\[
0\longrightarrow E(\mathbf Q)\otimes\mathbf Q_p
\longrightarrow S_f(\mathbf Q,V)
\longrightarrow W_p\longrightarrow0.
\tag{K}
\]
The left term has dimension 2.

**3. Local image is exactly one dimensional.** The plus subspace of
\(\oplus_{v\mid p}H_f^1(K_v,V)\) identifies with
\(H_f^1(\mathbf Q_p,V)\cong E(\mathbf Q_p)\otimes\mathbf Q_p\),
which has dimension 1. At a split p this is the diagonal of the two \(p\)-adic local spaces (for conjugate embeddings), and at an inert p the invariant line in the unique degree-two local space. Since any non-torsion rational point has a nonzero local p-adic logarithm, the localization of the MW subspace **already surjects** onto that line. All rational Selmer classes are + by step 1, so their localization has image *exactly* that one line.

**4. Snake lemma / subtraction of an MW class.** Restrict (K) to the kernel of localization. Any class in the quotient \(W_p\) has a Selmer lift \(s\); its p-local image belongs to the plus line and hence equals the localization of an MW class \(m\). Then \(s-m\in S_{0,p}(K,V)\), so the projection \(S_{0,p}(K,V)\to W_p\) is surjective. Its kernel consists exactly of MW classes with zero localization. The latter form the one-dimensional line generated by \(\eta_p\). This proves (*), (**), and (***).

**No appeal** was made to the p-adic BSD conjecture, a conjectural Euler-system primitivity, or the finiteness of \(\Sha(E/\mathbf Q)\).

## A sharper NO-GO for naive rank-two systems over K

The canonical relaxed-at-p Selmer structure \(\mathcal F_{\rm can}\) on \(T_pE\) over K has core rank 2, and its dual imposes the **strict** zero local condition at p (and the usual self-dual conditions elsewhere). But from (*) its rational dual Selmer space has **dimension at least 1 for every p**, even if Sha is zero. Consequently a putative primitive rank-two Euler/Kolyvagin system whose conclusion would be that the canonical strict dual Selmer is torsion/zero **cannot exist** in this setting, as stated. This is not just an unverified local hypothesis: it is an unavoidable geometric obstruction from the two rational MW generators and their dependent local logarithms.

The right quotient to control, instead, is
\[
\boxed{S_{0,p}(K,V)/\mathbf Q_p\eta_p\simeq W_p.}
\]
It is zero iff the p-primary Sha is *finite*. This requires a derived-height/Bockstein-corrected Euler-system mechanism targeting the quotient by the known rank-one geometric line; a plain primitive initial wedge of \(P\) and \(Q\) is insufficient.

## An actual all-p consequence and its limitation

If one could independently establish, for **all** good odd p outside an explicit finite set S, that the derived Selmer quotient \(S_{0,p}/\mathbf Q_p\eta_p\) is zero (for example via a theorem about rank-two generalized Kato classes), then Sha would be finite at each of those p, **but this alone STILL does not imply global finiteness**. One also needs:
- a single nonzero integral uniform bound on its orders or Fitting ideals to rule out infinitely many nonzero *finite* p-primary pieces;
- independent p-integral bounds at the finitely many exceptional primes, using existing evidence as appropriate.

Thus any claim that a rational nonvanishing theorem for all p immediately proves global finite Sha is *false*: rational Selmer dimensions control corank, not p-power orders.

## Relevance of known derived Kato classes

Castella–Hsieh (Forum of Mathematics Sigma 2022, Theorems A/B) show under explicit hypotheses that nonvanishing of a generalized Kato class forces
\(\dim_{\mathbf Q_p}\operatorname{Sel}_f(\mathbf Q,V_pE)=2\).
By (K), that is \(W_p=0\), and therefore by (*) strict Selmer over K is **exactly** \(\mathbf Q_p\eta_p\). This is the correct interpretation of the theorem for our rank-two curve: it kills the **extra rational strict classes**, not the geometric strict line and not finite p-primary Sha torsion.

The separate Burns–Kurihara–Sano 2024 *Darmon derivative* constructions are over **real abelian extensions of Q**; their interpolation and compatibility cannot be silently base-changed to the imaginary quadratic K used here. A genuine K-derived object needs new justification.
