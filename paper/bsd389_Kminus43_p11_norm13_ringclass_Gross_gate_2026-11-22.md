## 2. Exact ring-class group at conductor 121

For f prime to D_K and since O_K^×={±1} already lies in (Z/f)^×, the standard ring-class exact sequence gives

    Pic(O_f) ~= (O_K/f O_K)^× / (Z/fZ)^×,

where O_f = Z+f O_K. At p=11 the minimal polynomial X²−X+11 has distinct roots 0,1 modulo11. Their Hensel lifts modulo121 are

    r0 = 11,    r1 = 111,

as is verified by substituting into the polynomial and checking their difference is a unit. The Chinese remainder theorem therefore gives

    O_K / 121 O_K ~= (Z/121Z) x (Z/121Z),
    a+b omega          -> (a+11b, a+111b).

After dividing by the diagonal scalar units, the quotient is isomorphic to `(Z/121Z)^×` through the exact ratio

    rho(a+b omega) = (a+11b)/(a+111b) mod121.

Thus

    Pic(O_121) ~= (Z/121Z)^× ~= C_110.

Likewise Pic(O_11) ~= (Z/11Z)^× ~= C_10. The canonical maximal 11-primary quotient of Pic(O_121) is C_11. The first anticyclotomic 11-extension K_1/K is detected by this 11-primary quotient (the class number of K itself is 1).
