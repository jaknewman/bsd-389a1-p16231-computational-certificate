## 1. Setting and the first class-field comparison

Let
$$
E/\mathbf Q:y^2+y=x^3+x^2-2x,\qquad N=389,\qquad
E(\mathbf Q)\simeq\mathbf Z^2,
$$
and fix p≥5, p≠389, with surjective
$$
\rho_{E,p}:G_{\mathbf Q}\twoheadrightarrow
\Gamma_p=\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbf F_p).
$$
Put V=E[p], F=Q(E[p]), and S={p,389,∞}. By Néron–Ogg–Shafarevich, F is unramified outside S. The maximal exponent-p abelian quotient
$$
A_{F,S}:=\operatorname{Gal}(F_S^{\rm ab}/F)/p
$$
is finite as an Fp-vector space, by global class field theory (finite generation of S-units and finiteness of S-class groups). Here F_S^{ab} is the maximal abelian extension unramified outside places of F above S. Γ acts by conjugation on A and naturally on V.

**Theorem 1 (exact division-field class-field description).** There is a canonical Fp-linear isomorphism
$$
\boxed{
H^1(G_{\mathbf Q,S},V)\simeq
\operatorname{Hom}_{\Gamma_p}(A_{F,S},V).
}\tag{1}
$$
The proof is inflation–restriction for 1→G_{F,S}→G_{Q,S}→Γ→1. The central element z=−I∈Γ acts as −1 on V. Sah's lemma (central element acting by a scalar not 1) proves H^i(Γ,V)=0 for **all i≥0**, especially i=1,2. Since V is trivial on G_F, the middle restriction is an isomorphism to Γ-equivariant continuous homomorphisms from G_F^{ab}/p. The Selmer group injects into the left side, since at all primes q∉S the Kummer local condition is unramified. QED.

The exact rank-two global Kummer sequence then induces the **genuine independent-of-Sha construction**
$$
\boxed{
\Sha(E/\mathbf Q)[p]\hookrightarrow
\operatorname{Hom}_{\Gamma_p}(A_{F,S},V)/
\operatorname{res}\big(E(\mathbf Q)/pE(\mathbf Q)\big).
}\tag{2}
$$
The denominator is **two-dimensional**, since restriction is injective.

The central involution z=−I acts as −1 on V. Therefore every Γ-equivariant map A→V kills A^+=(1+z)A/2 and factors through
$$
A^-=(1-z)A/2,\quad
\operatorname{Hom}_\Gamma(A,V)=\operatorname{Hom}_\Gamma(A^-,V).
$$
This is an exact arithmetic **anti-invariant / relative class-field sieve**. It is not a proof that A^- has small p-rank. Geometrically F^{<z>} is the field generated by all x-coordinates of p-torsion points, since on our model z acts as (x,y)↦(x,−1−y).

When µ_p⊂F, the finite Kummer space
$$
F(S,p)=\{a\in F^\times/F^{\times p}:v_\mathfrak q(a)\equiv0\bmod p
\ \forall \mathfrak q\notin S\}
$$
satisfies the exact sequence
$$
0\to\mathcal O_{F,S}^\times/(\mathcal O_{F,S}^\times)^p
\to F(S,p)\to\mathrm{Cl}(\mathcal O_{F,S})[p]\to0.
$$
Its Kummer dual gives A_{F,S}. This is the *actual* arithmetic meaning of (1), not a bare Hecke-symbol lattice with an invented trivial Galois action.

Primary classical references: Néron–Ogg–Shafarevich; Sah's lemma as quoted by Nekovář, https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~jan.nekovar/pu/anticycl_v2.pdf §5.5; classical descent / S-unit class sequence as implemented in https://github.com/sagemath/sage/blob/develop/src/sage/rings/number_field/selmer_group.py ; references in Cremona–Fisher descent literature.

## 2. The stronger Borel reduction: a real isogeny Selmer injection over K of degree p+1

Choose a **one-dimensional subspace** \(C\subset V\). Let B⊂Γ be its stabilizer. Under full GL2,
$$
|B|=p(p-1)^2,\quad
[\Gamma:B]=p+1.
$$
Put
$$
\boxed{K=F^B,\quad[K:\mathbf Q]=p+1.}\tag{3}
$$
The cyclic subgroup C⊂E[p] is stable over K, so there are genuine K-rational degree-p isogenies
$$
\phi:E_K\longrightarrow E'=E_K/C,\qquad
\hat\phi:E'\longrightarrow E_K,\qquad
\hat\phi\phi=[p].
$$
The G_K-module W=E'[\hatφ] is canonically the quotient V/C (via φ), hence one-dimensional over Fp and carries the quotient Borel character \(\chi:B\to\mathbf F_p^\times\).

**Theorem 2 (genuine arithmetic injection — central new construction).**
$$
\boxed{
\Psi_p:\operatorname{Sel}_p(E/\mathbf Q)
\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Sel}_{\hat\phi}(E'/K).
}\tag{4}
$$

Here Sel_{\hatφ}(E'/K) denotes the Selmer group of the **dual isogeny** \(\hat\phi:E'\to E_K\); it lies in \(H^1(K,E'[\hat\phi])\).

**Proof of injectivity at the GLOBAL cohomology level.**
Compose restriction
\(H^1(G_{\mathbf Q,S},V)\to H^1(G_{K,S_K},V)\)
with the quotient map V→W=V/C. Suppose c is in the kernel, and restrict its cocycle further to \(G_{F,S}\). Since V is trivial on G_F, the restricted cocycle is a continuous homomorphism
\(h:G_{F,S}\to V\) which is Γ-equivariant by conjugation. Its W-valued projection is zero, so \(\operatorname{im}h\subset C\).

But Γ-equivariance makes \(\operatorname{im}h\) a Γ-stable Fp-subspace of V. The natural 2D representation of GL2(Fp) is irreducible, so a Γ-stable subspace contained in the proper line C must be zero. Thus h=0, and injectivity \(H^1(G_{\mathbf Q,S},V)\hookrightarrow H^1(G_{F,S},V)\) follows from H^1(Γ,V)=0 (Sah). Therefore c=0. QED global injectivity.

**Proof of local Selmer preservation.**
For every local place v of K above a rational place ℓ, and every local point P_ℓ∈E(Q_ℓ), choose a p-division Q∈E(\overline{K_v}) with pQ=P_ℓ. Its usual p-Kummer cocycle is \(gQ-Q\in E[p]\). Applying φ yields \(g\phi(Q)-\phi(Q)\in E'[\hat\phi]\), and \(\hat\phi(\phi(Q))=pQ=P_\ell\). This is **exactly** the local \(\hat\phi\)-Kummer cocycle attached to P_ℓ, regarded as a K_v-point. Therefore every p-Selmer class maps into the dual-isogeny Selmer conditions at every place. Combining with global injectivity proves (4). QED.

The exact Kummer quotient then supplies an ACTUAL finite arithmetic target independent of Sha:
$$
\boxed{
\Sha(E/\mathbf Q)[p]\hookrightarrow
\operatorname{Sel}_{\hat\phi}(E'/K)/
\Psi_p\big(E(\mathbf Q)/pE(\mathbf Q)\big).
}\tag{5}
$$
The denominator has Fp-dimension 2.

**Noncircular sufficient criterion.**
$$
\boxed{
\dim_{\mathbf F_p}\operatorname{Sel}_{\hat\phi}(E'/K)=2
\ \Longrightarrow\
\Sha(E/\mathbf Q)[p]=0
\ \Longrightarrow\
\Sha(E/\mathbf Q)[p^\infty]=0.}
\tag{6}
$$
The criterion is **sufficient but need NOT be necessary**: isogeny Selmer over K can contain many classes not descended from Q. The missing user-requested all-large-p implication would follow if (6)'s dimension-two hypothesis could be proved for every sufficiently large p; **no such uniform theorem is established here**.

This is a concrete upgrade over the previously sought *abstract* fixed integral Hecke matrix: the map (4) truly exists and has an explicitly arithmetic target, but **the target varies with p** and no fixed determinant D has been produced.

## 3. A smaller class-field realization of the target

In a basis with C=<e1>, B consists of upper triangular matrices \(\begin{psmallmatrix}a&b\\0&d\end{psmallmatrix}\), a,d∈Fp×, b∈Fp. The quotient character is \(\chi(B)=d\). Its kernel has order p(p−1), so the field
$$
L=F^{\ker\chi}
$$
has
$$
\boxed{[L:\mathbf Q]=p^2-1,\qquad
[L:K]=p-1,\quad\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)\simeq\mathbf F_p^\times.}
\tag{7}
$$
The Galois action on W is trivial over L, so because \([L:K]=p-1\) is prime to p, a second inflation–restriction gives the exact isomorphism
$$
\boxed{
H^1(G_{K,S_K},W)\simeq
\operatorname{Hom}_{\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)}
\left(A_{L,S_L},W\right),
}\tag{8}
$$
where \(A_{L,S_L}=\operatorname{Gal}(L^{ab}_{S_L}/L)/p\) is the finite exponent-p S-ramified class-field module. The true dual-isogeny Selmer is cut out by **additional local Kummer linear conditions** inside (8); dropping these conditions usually produces a much larger class-field target.

**Important subtlety:** Unlike F, L does **not necessarily contain** µ_p (in fact full GL2 implies det kerχ is all Fp×, so L does not contain µ_p). To compute A_{L,S_L} using multiplicative Kummer \(L^\times/L^{\times p}\), first pass to \(L(\mu_p)\) and take the appropriate cyclotomic eigenspace, or compute the ray class group over L directly. Do NOT naively identify A_L with the dual of L(S,p) without correcting for µ_p.
