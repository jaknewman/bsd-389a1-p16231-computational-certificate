# K=-3, p=16231: exact two-or-four generator gap for the genuine anticyclotomic Selmer module

Let K=Q(sqrt(-3)), p=16231 and Λ=Z_p[[T]], A=E[p∞], and
\[
X_\Delta=\mathrm{Sel}_{N^-=389}(K_\infty,A)^\vee,
\]
where the N^--ordinary local conditions are exactly those in Chida–Hsieh's original Proposition 1.9. Their theorem applies here and, by local comparison at the base K, gives the *proved* exact control

X_\Delta/TX_\Delta\cong \mathrm{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/K)^\vee.

The residual representation is irreducible; in particular E(K)[p]=0. The rank-two Kummer exact sequence, quadratic descent, and Pontryagin duality give

X_\Delta/TX_\Delta\cong
\mathbf Z_p^2\oplus A_{+,p}^\vee\oplus A_{-,p}^\vee,

A_{+,p}=\Sha(E/Q)[p^\infty],\quad

## Theorem (exact minimal generator formula)

Let \(\mu_\Lambda(X_\Delta)\) denote the minimal number of generators of the finitely generated module \(X_\Delta\) over the **local** ring Λ. Then
\[
\boxed{
\mu_\Lambda(X_\Delta)
=2+\dim_{\mathbf F_p}\Sha(E/Q)[p]
 +\dim_{\mathbf F_p}\Sha(E^{-3}/Q)[p].
}\tag{1}
\]
This formula is exact even if \Sha(E/Q)[p∞] is infinite, since the p-primary Sha is cofinitely generated.

**Proof:** Nakayama's lemma gives
\(\mu_\Lambda(X_\Delta)=\dim_{\mathbf F_p} X_\Delta/(p,T)X_\Delta\).
By control, this identifies with the \(\mathbf F_p\)-dimension of the p-torsion in the classical discrete Selmer group on K. By p-adic Kummer and quadratic descent for p odd, that dimension is exactly 2 plus the p-torsion dimensions of the two Sha summands. QED.

## Parity gap: two generators OR AT LEAST FOUR, never three

**Theorem (parity).** The two addends
\(\dim_{\mathbf F_p}\Sha(E/Q)[p]\) and
\(\dim_{\mathbf F_p}\Sha(E^{-3}/Q)[p]\) are individually EVEN at odd p.

**Proof:** For each rational elliptic curve the p-parity theorem of Tim and Vladimir Dokchitser (Annals of Mathematics 172 (2010), 567–596) asserts \((-1)^{\rho_p}=w(E)\), where \(\rho_p=\operatorname{rank}E(Q)+\operatorname{corank}_{Z_p}\Sha[p^\infty]\). Both curves have global root number +1; their Mordell–Weil ranks are respectively 2 and 0. Therefore both p-primary divisible coranks are even. In addition, the finite quotient of each p-primary Sha modulo its maximal divisible subgroup has a nondegenerate alternating Cassels–Tate pairing at odd p, so its p-rank is even (cyclic factors occur in identical pairs). Their sum, the p-rank of each Sha[p], is even. No total-Sha finiteness assumption is made. QED.

Therefore
\[
\boxed{\mu_\Lambda(X_\Delta)\in\{2,4,6,\dots\}.}\tag{2}
\]

**Corollary: the sharp THREE-generator sufficiency criterion**
\[
\boxed{
\mu_\Lambda(X_\Delta)\le3
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mu_\Lambda(X_\Delta)=2
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\Sha(E/Q)[p^\infty]=0
\ \text{AND}\
\Sha(E^{-3}/Q)[p^\infty]=0.
}\tag{3}
\]
The reverse direction is exact by (1); the forward direction uses parity and that a nonzero p-primary group contains nonzero p-torsion.

Thus, to prove the simultaneous vanishing of both primary Sha parts **it would suffice to construct an actual arithmetic presentation of the genuine \(X_\Delta\) generated by AT MOST THREE elements over Λ**. This can be more flexible than computing and proving that an explicit \(\mathrm{Fitt}_2\) minor is a unit, although it is *mathematically equivalent for this self-dual setup*.

### Explicit warning: initial Fitting and theta do not give the 3-generator bound

The already-proved universal abstract counterexample has
\(X_{\rm good}=\operatorname{coker}\operatorname{diag}(1,1,T^2,T^2)\)
with \(\mu=2\), and
\(X_{\rm bad}=\operatorname{coker}\operatorname{diag}(B,B)\) where
\(B=\begin{psmallmatrix}p&T\\T&0\end{psmallmatrix}\),
with \(\mu=4\). Both have the *identical* initial Fitting ideal (T⁴), with theta=T² a unit-leading quadratic series, and identical initial Fitting ideals at ALL finite layers. Both are Λ-torsion and have no finite Λ-submodule. Hence the 3-generator bound is **not** an automatic consequence of Castella–Hsieh's theta quadratic unit, Chida–Hsieh Iwasawa divisibility, Kim's initial-Fitting theorem, or the source-verified higher Fitting pseudo-equivalence.

The exact unresolved theorem is a rank-3 **integral generator bound** for the true \(X_\Delta\) at the height-two maximal ideal \((p,T)\). It must come from an independent Euler/Kolyvagin or arithmetic relation controlling the extra Sha directions, not from numerically repeating theta moments or MW local determinants.

No claim of a proven ≤3 generator bound, Sha=0 or complete BSD.
