# 389a1 — exact decomposition of the corrected Kato I1 ideal into the TRUE Sha ideal and the Kato-system index

**Date: 2026-11-24.** This is a proved ideal-theoretic consequence of the existing BSS theorems and the exact strict-to-full Selmer localization lemma (conditional on verifying that the particular normalized Kato Euler system and canonical Selmer structures satisfy the stated published hypotheses). It is **not** a proof of Sha(Q) finite, of Kato primitivity, or of strong BSD. No computations or new auxiliary primes are needed.

## 1. Precise theorem with quantifiers

Fix any sufficiently large odd prime p for which the BSS hypotheses and exact target identification have been established. Put O=Z_p, T=T_p(E), E=389a1, \(X_p=\operatorname{Sel}_{p^\infty}(E/\mathbf Q)^\vee\), \(Y_p=\Sha(E/\mathbf Q)[p^\infty]^\vee\), and \(X_p^0\) the dual of the p-strict Selmer group.

Let \(\mathfrak l_p=p^{t_p}O\) be the nonzero ideal generated by the coordinates of the p-adic localization of the saturated MW basis P,Q. The previous exact algebraic theorem gives
\[
\operatorname{Fitt}_{1,O}(X_p^0)
=\mathfrak l_p\,\operatorname{Fitt}_{2,O}(X_p)
=\mathfrak l_p\,\operatorname{Fitt}_{0,O}(Y_p).
\tag{1}
\]

Let \(\mathcal K_p=\operatorname{KS}^{1}(T,\mathcal F_{\mathrm{can}})\) be the inverse-limit BSS rank-one Kolyvagin-system module. BSS II arXiv:1805.08448v1, Theorem 5.25(i) (arXiv PDF p.36), proves \(\mathcal K_p\) is free of rank one over O. Fix any generator \(\kappa_p^{\rm prim}\). The **actual Kato-derived** system has a unique scalar
\[
\kappa_p^{\rm Kato}=a_p\,\kappa_p^{\rm prim},
\quad a_p\in O,
\]
whose ideal \((a_p)\) is independent of generator choice (only a unit changes a_p). \(a_p=0\) is allowed.

BSS II, Theorem 5.25(iii) and Corollary 6.18(ii) (arXiv v1), proves that the image ideals
\[
I_i(\kappa):=\sum_{\substack{n\ \text{admissible}\\\nu(n)=i}}
\operatorname{im}(\kappa_n)
\]
satisfy \(\ I_i(\kappa_p^{\rm prim})=\operatorname{Fitt}_i(X_p^0)\ \) for each i, since O is a DVR and \(\kappa_p^{\rm prim}\) is a basis. In particular
\[
I_1(\kappa_p^{\rm Kato})=(a_p)I_1(\kappa_p^{\rm prim})
=(a_p)\,\mathfrak l_p\,\operatorname{Fitt}_0(Y_p).
\tag{2}
\]

Taking the O-ideal quotient by \(\mathfrak l_p\), which is principal and nonzero, gives the **exact identity**
\[
\boxed{J_p:=(I_1(\kappa_p^{\rm Kato}):\mathfrak l_p)
=(a_p)\,\operatorname{Fitt}_{0,O}(Y_p).}
\tag{3}
\]

This is STRONGER than the previously established one-sided containment
\(J_p\subseteq\operatorname{Fitt}_0(Y_p)\); it is an algebraic factorization of its precise slack. It does NOT identify a_p or prove the right side is nonzero.

If \(Y_p\) is finite of order \(p^{e_p}\), then
\[
\boxed{v_p(J_p)=v_p(a_p)+e_p}
\]
with the convention \(v_p(0)=+\infty\). If Y_p has positive free O-rank, both \(\operatorname{Fitt}_0(Y_p)\) and \(J_p\) vanish. Thus:
\[
\boxed{J_p=O\Longleftrightarrow
  \bigl(\Sha(E/\mathbf Q)[p^\infty]=0\bigr)
  \land\bigl(\kappa_p^{\rm Kato}\ \text{is primitive in }\mathcal K_p\bigr).}
\]
This is a **logical equivalence**, not a proof of either conjunct.

The quantity \(v_p(a_p)\) measures the Kato-specific index independently of the true Sha order. It is not determined by the published existence of primitive systems.

## 2. Why an abstract primitive KS does not solve BSD

BSS/Mazur–Rubin provide a generator \(\kappa_p^{\rm prim}\) abstractly and guarantee
\(I_1(\kappa_p^{\rm prim})=\mathfrak l_p\operatorname{Fitt}_0(Y_p)\).
But knowing an abstract module is free rank1 DOES NOT determine the valuation of its generator's first-level components, because those depend precisely on the unknown dual Selmer Fitting ideal.

More sharply, an abstract primitive system may have \(I_1\) divisible by \(p^{t_p}\) even if Sha is zero; the local correction removes this mandatory factor, but Kato itself may have extra divisibility \(p^{v_p(a_p)}\). Nothing in the standard theorem says \(v_p(a_p)=0\) for all sufficiently large p.

**Algebraic countermodel to the overoptimistic converse**: let Y_p=0, t_p=0, and choose the perfectly valid nonprimitive Kolyvagin-system element \(\kappa_p^{\rm Kato}:=p\,\kappa_p^{\rm prim}\). Then \(J_p=pO\ne O\), even though Sha[p^\infty]=0. For any t_p the same conclusion holds after dividing the mandatory local factor.

Thus global BSD cannot be deduced merely from constructing the derivative morphism, checking core vertices, or proving the strict Fitting inclusion.

## 3. A single-admissible-prime arithmetic acceptance test (conditional on genuine class production)

By Definition 5.1 of BSS II, \(I_1\) is the ideal generated by the images of the components \(\kappa_{p,q}^{\rm Kato}\) for **one** auxiliary prime q (not the scalar classical two-factor Kurihara number). Equation (2) implies all such images lie in \(p^{t_p}O\).

Because O is a DVR and the ideal is a sum of these component-image ideals,
\[
\boxed{
J_p=O\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists\text{ BSS-admissible prime }q,\ \exists O\text{-linear functional }\varphi:
H^1_{\mathcal F(q)}(\mathbf Q,E[p^m])\to O/p^m
\text{ for some }m>t_p:
\ v_p\bigl(\varphi(\kappa_{p,q}^{\rm Kato})\bigr)=t_p.
}
\]
Here the valuation on O/p^m is defined as the smallest power of p dividing the residue and equals t_p when the chosen residue is not 0 mod p^{t_p+1}; the genuine BSS projective system/finite-level coefficient choices must be respected. Equivalent statement: some true \(\kappa_{p,q}\) has an image nonzero after division by p^{t_p} and reduction modulo p.

This is a **genuinely p-specific test** that, *if passed with cohomology and local-conditions certificates*, proves \(\Sha(E/\mathbf Q)[p^\infty]=0\) by the exact Fitting chain, even without a direct numerical upper bound on all of Sha. It requires ONE auxiliary Kolyvagin prime per p, but the crucial unproved theorem is that such a prime exists **for each sufficiently large p**.

**Crucial non-identification:** \(\kappa_{p,q}^{\rm Kato}\) is a Galois-cohomology class under BSS finite-singular descent, not the classical scalar first or second logarithmic moment of a Mazur–Tate polynomial. In particular the already computed two-factor Kurihara numbers \(\widetilde\delta_{q_1q_2}\) do NOT automatically furnish these cohomology classes or their functional evaluations. Replacing one by the other needs a new explicit reciprocity comparison, which is precisely a genuinely arithmetic research task.

## 4. A fixed D and the exact uniform criterion

If a single **nonzero integer** D has \((D)\subseteq J_p\) for every sufficiently large p and all remaining primes are separately handled, then
\[
v_p(a_p)+e_p\le v_p(D)\qquad
\text{whenever }e_p=\operatorname{length}_O(Y_p)<\infty.
\]
When p does not divide D, \((D)=O\) and (3) forces both a_p unit and Y_p=0. If D is supported on independently certified Sha-zero primes, global Sha vanishes after checking them.

This reveals that a uniform **Kato** certificate is strictly stronger than a certificate for Sha alone: it bounds the exceptional prime divisors of *both* the genuine Sha group and the Kato derivative index. The latter is a new and distinct source of p-divisibility. A uniform D supported on known primes is desirable but not yet constructed.

## 5. Research verdict — precise final obstruction, not a computational substitute

The core-vertex and strict/full local comparison steps are sufficiently understood (given the prior checks of source hypotheses for the large-p tail) to reduce global Sha to a single genuinely new assertion: for almost every p, the Kato-specific system and the true p-Selmer Fitting index have jointly unit corrected content, or provide a single uniform finite-support integer D.

The **exact identity (3)** is proved, but it does not establish that the right side is 1. That is the boundary between verified theory and the missing BSD argument. Producing additional modular-symbol histograms, Smith matrices, or finite-level prime lists will not resolve this uniform cohomological primitivity problem unless they are accompanied by an explicit reciprocity theorem converting them into a genuine BSS component of \(\kappa_p\).
