# E389a1: exact class-group/Kurihara degree identity with a local Wieferich correction

Date: 21 November 2026. This note **corrects** a too-strong assertion
that d_p=1+dim Sha[p] unconditionally for every good nonanomalous p.
The correct proven theorem has an explicit local binary correction
epsilon_p in {0,1}; parity removes it when extracting the MINIMAL
UNIT KURIHARA DEGREE. It still does NOT prove a uniform p-tail bound.

## Setup and authentic primary sources

E/Q: y^2+y=x^3+x^2-2x, conductor/minimal discriminant 389,
MW group Z^2, global root number +1, split multiplicative I_1 at 389.
Let p>=5, p not in {97,389}, p a NONANOMALOUS good prime:
p does not divide #E(F_p), i.e. a_p !=1 mod p.
Assume the established full mod-p image GL2(F_p). Put
  V=E[p], K=Q(E[p]), G=Gal(K/Q)=GL2(F_p),
  C=Hom_G(Cl(K)/p,V),  d_p=dim_Fp C,
  S=Sel(Q,E[p]),
  S^str=ker(S -> H^1(Q_p,V)),
  s_p=dim_Fp Sha(E/Q)[p] in {0,2,4,...},
  epsilon_p=dim_Fp image(S -> H^1(Q_p,V)) in {0,1}.

Primary proof input:
- D. Prasad and S. Shekhar, *Relating Tate-Shafarevich group of
  an elliptic curve with class group*, arXiv:1912.12928v1,
  Theorem 4.1 and the proof, especially the sentence saying that
  when the exceptional set T is EMPTY, C is naturally isomorphic
  to their "everywhere-unramified" group R_p(Q,E[p]).
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.12928
- A. Ray and T. Weston, *Class group statistics for torsion fields
  generated by elliptic curves*, arXiv:2204.09757v2, Theorems
  2.1 and 2.3: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2204.09757
- C.-H. Kim, Theorem 1.8; F. Castella--T. Sano, Theorem B,
  for the proven all-good-prime equality between smallest unit
  Kurihara degree mu_p and dim Sel(Q,E[p]):
  https://preprint.press.jhu.edu/ajm/sites/default/files/AJM-kim.pdf
  https://web.math.ucsb.edu/~castella/Kurihara.pdf

**Important source discrepancy:** the arXiv 2019 version of
Prasad--Shekhar Theorem 4.1 prints the upper bound
  d_p <= dim(S)+#T,
whereas Ray--Weston 2024 Theorem 2.3 attributes the stronger
  d_p <= dim(S)-1+#I
to the *published* Prasad--Shekhar Theorem 4.2.
We have NOT independently authenticated that stronger bound

Under this note's assumptions, the precise local correction

## Theorem 1: class group is exactly the strict mod-p Selmer

There is a natural F_p-linear isomorphism
  ** C ~= S^str. **

PROOF: Since G contains the central scalar 2I acting as
multiplication by 2 on V, Sah's lemma gives
H^1(G,V)=H^2(G,V)=0, hence by inflation--restriction:
  H^1(Q,V) ~= H^1(K,V)^G.
The globally everywhere-unramified subgroup on K is C
by class field theory.

We compare its local conditions after inflation:

(1) At a good place l != p, E[p] is unramified at l
and the inflation preimage of the K_w-unramified subgroup
is the Q_l-unramified group, identical to the elliptic
Kummer subgroup for good l!=p.

(2) At l=389 the Tate uniformization gives the inertia
image generated by a unipotent U=(1 1;0 1) of order p,
since v_389(Delta)=1. Its group cohomology H^1(<U>,V)
is one dimensional, represented by e2 modulo (U-I)V=e1.
Arithmetic Frobenius Frob_389 conjugates U to U^389
and acts trivially on e2 modulo e1. Therefore its
conjugation action on the H^1 line is multiplication by
389^{-1} modulo p. As p !=97,389 and p>=5, 389 !=1
mod p. So the invariant part is ZERO: any class
unramified over K is unramified already over Q_389.
Furthermore H^1_ur(Q_389,V)=0: V^I=mu_p line and
Frob acts on it by 389 !=1 modulo p. Thus its local
class is zero, precisely the Kummer condition because
E(Q_389)/p=0 (torus order 388, Tamagawa 1).

(3) At p, good supersingular inertia has tame image
of order prime to p, so H^1(image(Ip),V)=0. In the
good ordinary case the inertia image lies in an
upper triangular Borel with tame cyclotomic quotient
F_p^* and possible unipotent p-kernel U. If the
p-kernel is absent, H^1(inertia image,V)=0 by
prime-to-p order. If present, the H^1(U,V) line
transforms by a^{-1} under the tame diagonal
diag(a,1); averaging gives zero invariants
since p>=5 and tame cyclotomic has full image.
Thus the inflating class is unramified over Q_p.
The Q_p-unramified group H^1_ur(Q_p,V) is zero:
for good supersingular inertia invariants vanish,
and for nonanomalous good ordinary p any inertia
invariant line has Frobenius eigen a_p mod p
which is not 1. Therefore its localization at p
is exactly zero.

(4) At the real place p is odd so H^1(R,V)=0.
The above shows C maps INTO the p-strict elliptic
Selmer. Conversely any p-strict elliptic Selmer
class is Kummer-unramified away from p and 389,
zero at p, and zero at 389 since p∤388.
Its restriction to K is everywhere unramified,
so it lies in C. These constructions are inverse.

This matches Prasad--Shekhar's theorem when their
exceptional bad-prime set T is empty. QED.

## Theorem 2: exact local correction and sharp classification

Because E(Q) has rank exactly 2 and trivial torsion,
Kummer gives
  dim S=2+s_p.
Good nonanomalous p gives local Kummer dimension exactly 1,
so epsilon_p in {0,1}. By Theorem 1:
  **d_p = 2 + s_p - epsilon_p.**

The p-Selmer parity theorem and the perfect alternating
Cassels--Tate pairing on the reduced Sha ensure s_p even
(this does not presuppose finite Sha).

Consequently:
  **Sha[p] = 0 iff d_p <=2**.
More precisely:
  d_p=1 or 2 iff s_p=0;
  d_p=3 or 4 iff s_p=2;
  d_p=5 or 6 iff s_p=4;
  in general s_p=2*ceil(d_p/2)-2.

If the global MW point P or Q is locally primitive
at p, then epsilon_p=1, and the simpler
d_p=1+s_p holds at that SPECIFIC p.
If Sha[p]=0 but both global MW generators are
locally p-divisible, then epsilon_p=0 and d_p=2.
This is an elliptic-Wieferich-type local defect.
Nothing here proves epsilon_p=1 for ALL large p.

## Theorem 3: cross-theory exact identity with Kurihara

The earlier established Kim + Castella--Sano all-good-prime
theorem gives the smallest auxiliary factor count mu_p
for a p-UNIT normalized Kurihara coefficient:
  mu_p=dim Sel(Q,E[p])=2+s_p, an EVEN integer.

Combining with Theorem 2:
  **mu_p = 2*ceil(d_p/2)**,
  **epsilon_p = mu_p - d_p = d_p mod 2**,
  **dim_Fp Sha[p] = 2*ceil(d_p/2)-2**.

Interpretation: an ODD V-isotypic class-group multiplicity
signals epsilon_p=1, and an EVEN one epsilon_p=0.
The entire mod-p Sha dimension can be read from d_p
without separately knowing epsilon_p.

This is an exact bridge between class groups, global
elliptic Kummer localization and the Kurihara minimal
degree for good nonanomalous p !=97. It supplies NO
uniform upper bound on d_p or mu_p by itself.

## A finer necessary divisibility, including the local correction

The G-equivariant surjection
  Cl(K)/p -> E[p]^{(+d_p)}
proved by the elementary irreducible-quotient argument
in the companion full-division-field note shows
  p^(2d_p) | h(K).

The tame subfield F=K^{diag(1,a)} receives an injective
covector descent of d_p independent class homomorphisms,
so
  p^(d_p) | h(F).

By d_p=2+s_p-epsilon_p:

- if Sha[p]!=0, then s_p>=2, so d_p>=3;
  hence p^6|h(K) and p^3|h(F) (the earlier bounds);
- if additionally epsilon_p=0, then d_p>=4, so
  p^8|h(K) and p^4|h(F);
- if Sha[p]=0, then d_p is 1 or 2 depending
  on the *local MW primitivity*. Even though Sha=0,
  p|h(F) always, and if BOTH saturated global
  MW generators vanish in E(Q_p)/p then
  d_p=2 and already p^2|h(F), p^4|h(K).

The last bullet explains why the 2024 Ray--Weston
quoted universal upper d<=1 for rank-two,
Sha-trivial curves must NOT be used without verifying
its actual published hypotheses/version. The
local elliptic-Wieferich phenomenon is an independent
possible source of a second class-group copy.

## The exact requirement to obtain finite global prime support

For every sufficiently large NONANOMALOUS p, proving
  d_p<=2
is equivalent to proving Sha[p]=0 by the theorem.
But non-CM elliptic curves can have anomalous primes,
and their finiteness for a fixed curve is not known.
Consequently an all-nonanomal tail result, even if
obtained, is NOT by itself a global finite-prime-support
theorem; a separate argument must cover anomalous
p, for example the earlier sufficient class-group
rank bound for F_p at anomalous primes or a
different p-uniform Selmer/Euler-system bound.

No known unconditional bound in the cited
Prasad--Shekhar, Ray--Weston, Kim or Castella--Sano
results implies the required eventual d_p<=2.
