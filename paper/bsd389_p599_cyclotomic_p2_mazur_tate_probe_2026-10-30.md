## A different group ring at much smaller conductor

Let E=389a1, p=599, and S(a/m) be the true plus Néron-normalized modular symbol. Let m=p^2=358801. The finite-level Mazur–Tate element is

  theta_(p^2)=sum_(a mod p^2, p not dividing a) S(a/p^2) sigma_a.

It lies in the group ring over (Z/p^2)^*. This group splits canonically as Delta x Gamma, where Delta has order p-1 and Gamma is cyclic of order p generated by gamma=1+p mod p^2.

For a coprime to p, the Teichmueller element omega(a) is a^p mod p^2; its p-1 power is 1. There is a unique k(a) in F_p such that

  a = omega(a) gamma^k(a) mod p^2,

and the precise Fermat-quotient formula is

  k(a) = -(a^(p-1)-1)/p mod p.

To compute it, first reduce a^(p-1) mod p^2, subtract 1, and divide by p. Since the numerator is divisible by p, the formula is unambiguous. The identity has been verified in independent plain Python exhaustive loops for all 20 units at p=5, all 42 units at p=7, and all 358202 units at p=599, including compatibility with negation.

Project to the tame-trivial part and identify gamma with 1+T in Fp[T]/(T^p). The coefficient of T^j, j=0,1,2, of this projection is exactly

  C_j = sum_(a units mod p^2) S(a/p^2) binomial(k(a),j) mod p.

This has only p(p-1)=358202 terms, or exactly p(p-1)/2=179101 representatives using S(-a)=S(a) and k(-a)=k(a). It is **not** the same as the Kurihara coefficient from q1=6912461 and q2=13963889.

## Hecke identities supply a complete low-cost consistency audit

The standard weight-two Hecke relation at p, with r=b/p, reads

  a_p S(b/p) = S(b) + sum_(j=0)^(p-1) S((b+j*p)/p^2).

Since S(b)=S(0) by integer periodicity, the fibre bucket at fixed b mod p must equal

  bucket_b = a_p S(b/p) - S(0).
