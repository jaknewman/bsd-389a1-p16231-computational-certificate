# Integral cancellation in the derived Kato formula (2026-11-06)

For good p>=5 with p not dividing #E(F_p), each local formal logarithm log_p(R) of a rational point R belongs to p Z_p: multiply R by the p-unit N_p=#E(F_p) to reach the formal subgroup, then divide its logarithm by N_p. Write tilde_eta=(log_p(Q)/p) P-(log_p(P)/p) Q, an integral Mordell-Weil tensor. Castella-Hsieh Theorem 5.5 gives kappa=C_p*e_p*(theta2/H_p)*eta, where e_p=(1-alpha_p/p)/(1-alpha_p^(-1)) and eta=p*tilde_eta. Hence kappa=C_p*(p*e_p)*(theta2/H_p)*tilde_eta, with p*e_p=(p-alpha_p)/(1-alpha_p^(-1)), a p-unit when p is nonanomalous. At p=16231, N_p=16104, alpha_p=128 mod p, p*e_p=8306 mod p, theta2=3739 mod p, and (p*e_p)*theta2=6231 mod p. Thus the formerly flagged p^(-1) Euler denominator is compensated by the *universal local p-divisibility of MW elliptic logarithms* in this case. Still open: the valuation of C_p/H_p, primitivity of tilde_eta in the saturated MW log-kernel, integral comparison with finite Sha, and full complex BSD. None of the earlier certificates was rerun. Source: Castella-Hsieh Forum Math Sigma (2022) Theorem 5.5.
