## 3. Poitou–Tate gives an extra genuine global cohomology class

Let V=E[5]. The usual self-dual 5-Selmer structure on V is denoted \(\mathcal F\). Its base Selmer has F5-dimension exactly 2, because rank E(Q)=2 and Sha(E/Q)[5]=0:
\[

At q=q2, let \(H_f^1(\mathbf Q_q,V)\) be the local Kummer condition and \(H_s^1=H^1/H_f^1\) the singular quotient. As q!=5 and E has good reduction, \(H_f^1=H_{ur}^1\). By local Tate duality and the Weil pairing,
\[
\dim_{\mathbf F_5}H_f^1=2,\qquad
\dim_{\mathbf F_5}H_s^1=2,

and the perfect pairing between \(H_s^1\) and \(H_f^1\) identifies the transpose of localisation of S with the map from H_s^1 into the dual base Selmer.

Define \(S^{rel(q)}\) by keeping ALL standard Selmer conditions away from q, and imposing **no** local condition at q. Poitou–Tate (change-of-Selmer-conditions exact sequence) yields
\[
0\to S\to S^{rel(q)}
  \to H_s^1(\mathbf Q_q,V)
  \xrightarrow{\delta}S^\vee.
\]
The rank of \(\delta\) equals the rank of the localisation of S into \(H_f^1(\mathbf Q_q,V)\), which the explicit P,Q computation shows is **ONE**. Consequently
\[
\boxed{\dim_{\mathbf F_5}S^{rel(q)}

There is therefore a genuine global class \(c\in H^1(\mathbf Q,V)\) outside the base Selmer, with possible ramification only at q. This class is guaranteed by Poitou–Tate; no invented Selmer element or numerical matrix-to-Sha identification is involved.

## 4. Why the third class becomes Selmer in each K_r

At q=q2, the extension \(K_r/\mathbf Q\) is **totally tamely ramified of degree 25**. Since E has good reduction and q!=5, V=E[5] is unramified at q.

For any local cohomology class \(c_q\in H^1(\mathbf Q_q,V)\), its restriction to inertia \(I_q\) is a continuous homomorphism into the exponent-5 module V. The 5-primary tame inertia quotient is \(\mathbf Z_5(1)\). Since \(I(K_{r,w})\) has index 25 in \(I_q\), restriction of this homomorphism to \(I(K_{r,w})\) is zero. Thus **every** local class at q becomes unramified after restriction to \(K_{r,w}\), and therefore meets the good-reduction local 5-Kummer condition over K.

At every other finite place, the class c was already locally Kummer, and restricting a Kummer class remains Kummer. Hence
\[
\operatorname{res}_{K_r/\mathbf Q}S^{rel(q)}
\subseteq\operatorname{Sel}_5(E/K_r)^{G_r}.
\]

Since \(K_r/\mathbf Q\) is Galois, E[5](K_r)=0, and the inflation–restriction sequence makes \(H^1(\mathbf Q,V)\to H^1(K_r,V)\) **injective**. Hence
\[
\boxed{\dim_{\mathbf F_5}\operatorname{Sel}_5(E/K_r)^{G_r}\ge3.}

But E(K_r)=E(Q) with no 5-torsion, so its Kummer classes form only a TWO-dimensional subspace of the G_r-invariant Selmer group. The extra class cannot be Kummer, by injectivity of global restriction. Its image in Sha(E/K_r)[5] is nonzero and G_r-invariant:
\[
\boxed{\Sha(E/K_r)[5]^{G_r}\ne0.}

This proves the main theorem without assuming or directly computing the global Sha group.
