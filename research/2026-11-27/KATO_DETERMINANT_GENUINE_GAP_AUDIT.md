# Rank-two Kato determinant audit: the exact integral map exists, but its primitivity is not automatic

Date: 27 November 2026. Status: primary-source audit and a precise arithmetic test specification. **NOT a proof of H2, Sha=0 for untested p, or complex BSD.**

## The actual constructed map (not a conjectured formal contraction)

For the Tate lattice \(T_p(E)\), the complex \(C_\infty\) of Burns–Kurihara–Sano (BKS, J. Math. Soc. Japan 76 (2024), §7) is a genuine global arithmetic complex over \(\Lambda=\mathbf Z_p[[\Gamma]]\). Its specialization \(C_0=C_\infty\otimes_\Lambda^{\mathbf L}\mathbf Z_p\) is a perfect complex. BKS §7.3 constructs a commutative diagram
\[
\det_\Lambda(C_\infty)\longrightarrow I^{r-1}H^1
\quad\downarrow\qquad\qquad\qquad\downarrow
\quad\det_{\mathbf Z_p}(C_0)\overset{\Pi_x}{\longrightarrow}\bigwedge^r_{\mathbf Z_p}H_0^1,
\]
whose second route into the first-derivative space is the true Bockstein map. Here \(r=\operatorname{rank}E(\mathbf Q)=2\), so the *cyclotomic Bockstein degree* is \(r-1=1\); **it is not a two-auxiliary-prime Kurihara contraction**, which lives in a different multivariable augmentation algebra. No canonical identification of these two objects is supplied by this diagram.

Let \(z_\infty\) denote the zeta element in the rationalized determinant line. BKS Theorem 7.8 applies assuming the standing freeness/Hypothesis 2.2 and **Conjecture 7.1 (the relevant Iwasawa main conjecture)**, as made explicit by §7.3. Under these assumptions its construction gives
\[
\eta_{\mathrm{Kato},x}:=\Pi_x(N_\infty(z_\infty))\in\bigwedge^2 H_0^1,
\qquad
\boxed{\mathbf Z_p\eta_{\mathrm{Kato},x}=
\# H^2(\mathbf Z_S,T_pE)_{\rm tors}\;\bigwedge^2_{\mathbf Z_p}H_0^1}.
\]
BKS Theorem 7.8(d) also identifies the *Bockstein image* lattice with
\[
v_\xi\Bigl(\prod_{\ell\in S\setminus\{\infty\}}L_\ell\Bigr)
\#\Sha(E/\mathbf Q)[p^\infty]\,{\rm Tam}(E)\,
\#E(\mathbf Q)_{\rm tors}^{-2}\;
R_\omega^{\rm Boc}
\]
up to \(\mathbf Z_p^\times\), whenever the terms are defined under the standing hypotheses.

In particular, at a fixed admissible p, once the main conjecture and normalizations are legitimately identified:
\[
\boxed{\text{\(\eta_{\mathrm{Kato},x}\) primitive}
\iff H^2(\mathbf Z_S,T_pE)_{\rm tors}=0.}
\]
This is an **exact criterion** but not the desired unconditional primitivity proof.

## The actual independent missing measurement

A verifiable, non-circular certificate for each remaining p must compute an **integral global cochain complex** (or a rigorously equivalent arithmetic presentation) and its **Smith/Fitting valuation** on the H² torsion part. A conjectural formal division by an Euler zero does not compute that valuation.

An acceptable new all-prime theorem would exhibit a bounded, fixed integral arithmetic presentation \(D_E\) independent of p (or a suitable perfect integral complex with a proven specialization theorem), and prove its torsion-determining maximal minors generate the unit ideal in \(\mathbf Z[1/389]\); **one must first construct and verify such a complex**, not assume its existence. If an integer minor equals ±389^k (or more generally a unit of \(\mathbf Z[1/389]\)), the minor ideal is p-unit for every p ≠389, excluding all ordinary and supersingular p simultaneously. But a random finite matrix of local point reductions, heights, or modular symbols is not a valid substitute for a global Selmer-complex presentation.

A weaker *valid* route is a p-dependent integral presentation at the as-yet-untested prime, with certified Smith pivots proving H² torsion=0. This yields a local p-primary theorem but **not** an infinite family of primes.

## Elementary verification of the index mechanism

The two-term \(\mathbf Z_p\)-complex
\[
C_{p,e}:\quad \mathbf Z_p^4
\xrightarrow{\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&0&p^e&0\\0&0&0&p^e\end{smallmatrix}\right)}
\mathbf Z_p^2
\]
has \(H^1\simeq\mathbf Z_p^2\) generated by its first two basis vectors and \(H^2\simeq(\mathbf Z/p^e)^2\). The order of \(H^2_{\rm tors}\) and the (nonzero) maximal-minor ideal are both \(p^{2e}\). The determinant line maps into \(\bigwedge^2 H^1\) with index \(p^{2e}\), which is nonunit when \(e>0\). This shows **why constructing the Bockstein determinant does not prove it is a primitive generator**.

The code \`verification/bsd389_kato_determinant_lattice_audit.py\` checks this exact lattice mechanism for several p, e. It is a test of algebra, **not** a claimed computation of \(H^2(\mathbf Z_S,T_p(E))\) for 389a1.

## Hypotheses not to erase

- Castella–Sano (2026) proves the refined Kurihara nonvanishing/main conjecture in specified good ordinary and squarefree-conductor supersingular cases, subject to their residual assumptions. This supplies a possible route to the main-conjecture hypothesis in the BKS theorem but does **not** prove primitivity of the determinant.
- BKS's generalized Perrin–Riou comparison with the **complex** leading term remains conjectural in the rank-two situation; BKS Theorem 7.6 explicitly assumes the corresponding conjecture and nonvanishing of the Bockstein regulator.
- The BKS \(\Gamma\)-augmentation Bockstein is **not** identified merely by notation with the completely mixed \(\ell_1\ell_2\)-Kurihara coefficient. Such a comparison requires a new integral theorem if used to prove H2.
- Finite computations of ordinary \(p\)-adic L jets for 309 primes do not establish a global determinant unit for all p.

**Exact outstanding theorem target:** construct a fully integral presentation of the *actual* rank-two Selmer complex for E=389a1 with a certified uniform unit minor away from the bad prime, or construct a global derived Kato-to-Kurihara comparison whose p-primitivity is shown independently of the unknown torsion. Neither certificate is currently available.
